Frequently Asked Questions

How does manual payment verification work?

You can pay via bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or Bank Transfer. After sending the payment, enter your Transaction ID in the checkout screen and our admins will verify and activate your portal.

Can I upgrade my tournament plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time through your Organization Billing Center, and new limits are applied instantly upon verification.

Can I connect my school's custom domain?

Custom domains are supported on Single Pro, Large Tournament, and Yearly Membership plans. You can point your DNS CNAME to our servers.
