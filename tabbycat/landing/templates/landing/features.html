Platform Features

Discover how Tabbycat manages every phase of tournament orchestration with high reliability.

Automated Pairing Engine

Uses mathematical matching algorithms (Hungarian minimum-cost maximum bipartite matching) to generate draws. Handles power-pairing, side allocation equality, institutional conflict rules, and adjudicator clash detection automatically.

Private URLs & Digital Ballots

Generate secure personalized URLs for every judge and participant. Adjudicators can submit scores and feedback directly from their mobile phones without logging in or downloading an app.

Multi-Tenancy & Subdomains

Isolate data across societies, clubs, and circuits. Each organization gets an independent portal, subdomain routing, custom logo, custom navigation, and isolated member permissions.

Real-Time Break & Tab Engine

Instantaneous calculations for open breaks and categorical breaks (ESL, EFL, Novice, High School). Configurable speaker score metrics, rankings, trimmed averages, and printable certificate sheets.
